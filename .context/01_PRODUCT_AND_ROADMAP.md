# Product Strategy & Sprint Roadmap

## Core Vision & Positioning
- **Brand**: Datcha Real Estate
- **Tagline**: "Homes. Investments. A Brighter Tomorrow."
- **Core Value Proposition**: "More than properties. A lifestyle by the Red Sea."
- **Primary Domain**: datcharealestate.com / www.datcharealestate.com
- **Target Audience**: Local families & high-net-worth international investors across Hurghada, El Gouna, Sahl Hasheesh, Makadi Bay, and Soma Bay (Red Sea, Egypt).
- **Format**: Single-Page Architectural & Brokerage Portfolio (Smooth Anchor Navigation). Strictly no real-time listing feeds or heavy search engines.

## Key Offerings / Asset Classes
1. **Prime Land Plots** (Strategic parcels, legal title, appreciation)
2. **Sea-View Apartments** (Lifestyle units, rental yields, Red Sea views)
3. **Residential Villas & Homes** (Private gated communities, starter to estates)
4. **Commercial Shops & Showrooms** (High-footfall tourism corridors)
5. **Business Offices** (Grade-A regional enterprise & HQ spaces)

## Service Pillars
1. **Local Expertise** (15+ years navigating Red Sea properties)
2. **Transparent Terms** (Zero hidden fees, full disclosure)
3. **Multilingual Team** (English, Arabic, Russian, German)
4. **Legal Support** (End-to-end title verification to contract signing)
